Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 April 2026

PlayARM ("we", "our", or "us") is an educational ARM assembly simulator. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Email address — when you create an account via email/password, Google, or GitHub sign-in (powered by Firebase Authentication).
  • Display name / nickname — a nickname you choose inside the app, stored in Firestore.
  • ARM programs you save — assembly code you explicitly save to the cloud is stored in Firestore linked to your account.
  • Exercise progress — on mobile, progress is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and is never sent to our servers.
  • App preferences — theme, playback speed, font size, and haptics settings are stored locally on your device only.

We do not collect location data, device identifiers, payment information, or any sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and identify your saved programs across devices.
  • To display your nickname inside the app.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and cloud database. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Expo / EAS — app build and delivery infrastructure.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties.

4. Data Retention

Your account and saved programs are retained until you delete your account. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us (see below) — we will permanently delete all your data within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

PlayARM is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Firebase enforces encryption in transit and at rest. We follow security best practices, but no system is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at sardarziyatxanov2@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
